[english]
Gethsemane

1. Jesus climbed the hill to the Garden still; His steps were heavy and slow.
Love and a prayer took Him there to the place only He could go.

Chorus:
Gethsemane! Jesus loves me,
So He went willingly to Gethsemane.

2. He felt all that was sad, wicked, or bad, all the pain we would ever know.
While His friends were asleep, He fought to keep His promise made long ago.

Chorus:
Gethsemane! Jesus loves me,
So He went willingly to Gethsemane.

Bridge:
The hardest thing that ever was done,
The greatest pain that ever was known,
The biggest battle that ever was won—
This was done by Jesus!
The fight was won by Jesus!

Chorus:
Gethsemane! Jesus loves me,
So He gave this gift to me in Gethsemane.

Gethsemane! Jesus loves me,
So He gives this gift to me from Gethsemane.

客西馬尼

1. 耶穌靜靜地往客西馬尼，
沉重緩慢地走去。
愛和禱告領祂去
只有祂能去的那裡。

Chorus:
客西馬尼，耶穌愛你，
所以甘心樂意往客西馬尼。

2. 祂感受了傷心，邪惡不平，
我們所有痛苦經歷。
當祂朋友睡去，祂盡全力
保守許下的約定。

Chorus:
客西馬尼，耶穌愛你，
所以甘心樂意往客西馬尼。

Bridge:
一切中最困難的事情，
一切中最痛苦的經歷，
一切戰爭最偉大的勝利
耶穌都已承擔，
耶穌贏了這戰役！

Chorus:
客西馬尼，耶穌愛你，
祂給了我這大禮，在客西馬尼。

客西馬尼，耶穌愛你，

Kè xī mǎ ní

Zhǐyǒu Tā néng qù dì nàlǐ.

Chorus:
Kè xī mǎ ní, Yēsū ài nǐ,
Suǒyǐ gānxīn lèyì wǎng Kè xī mǎ ní.

2. Tā gǎnshòule shāngxīn, xié'è bùpíng,

Dāng Tā péngyǒu shuì qù, Tā jìn quánlì
Bǎoshǒu xǔ xià di yuēdìng.

Chorus:
Kè xī mǎ ní, Yēsū ài nǐ,
Suǒyǐ gānxīn lèyì wǎng Kè xī mǎ ní.
