[english]
My Heavenly Father Loves Me

1. Whenever I hear the song of a bird
Or look at the blue, blue sky,
Whenever I feel the rain on my face
Or the wind as it rushes by,
Whenever I touch a velvet rose
Or walk by our lilac tree,
I'm glad that I live in this beautiful world
Heav'nly Father created for me.

2. He gave me my eyes that I might see
The color of butterfly wings.
He gave me my ears that I might hear
The magical sound of things.
He gave me my life, my mind, my heart:
I thank him rev'rently
For all his creations, of which I'm a part.
Yes, I know Heav'nly Father loves me.

[chinese]
我天父愛我

1. 每當我聽見，小鳥歌唱，
或抬頭望見藍天，
每當小雨點，滑過臉龐，
或微風輕輕拂面，
每當我輕觸柔柔玫瑰，
或漫步小樹邊，
我感謝天父為我創造世界，
賜我這美麗奇妙恩典。

2. 祂賜我眼睛，使我看見
蝴蝶的彩色翅膀，
祂賜我雙耳，使我聽見
奇妙的各種聲音，
祂賜我生命，我心，我靈，
我虔誠地感謝，
我感謝天父創造所有一切，
讓我知道我天父愛我。

[pinyin]
Wǒ Tiānfù ài wǒ

1. Měi dāng wǒ tingjiàn, xiǎoniǎo gēchàng,
Huò táitóu wàngjiàn lántiān,
Měi dāng xiǎo yǔdiǎn, huáguò liǎnpáng,
Huò wéifēng qīngqīng fú miàn,
Měi dāng wǒ qīng chù róuróu méiguī,
Huò mànbù xiǎoshù biān,
Wǒ gǎnxiè Tiānfù wèi wǒ chuàngzào shìjiè,
Cì wǒ zhè měilì qímiào ēndiǎn.

2. Tā cì wǒ yǎnjīng, shǐ wǒ kànjiàn
Húdié di cǎisè chìbǎng,
Tā cì wǒ shuāngěr, shǐ wǒ tīngjiàn
Qímiào di gè zhǒng shēngyīn,
Tā cì wǒ shēngmìng, wǒ xīn, wǒ líng,
Wǒ qiánchéng di gǎnxiè,
Wǒ gǎnxiè Tiānfù chuàngzào suǒyǒu yīqiè,
Ràng wǒ zhīdào wǒ Tiānfù ài wǒ.
